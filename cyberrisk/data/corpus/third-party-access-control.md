# Third-Party Access Control (synthetic guidance)

Vendors receive the minimum access needed, for the shortest time needed. Access is granted to named individuals, approved by the business owner and removed when the engagement ends.

Remote vendor sessions pass through a monitored gateway and are recorded for privileged work. Network segments reachable by vendors are separated from internal systems.

Quarterly access reviews compare the vendor account list with active contracts. Orphaned accounts are disabled immediately and logged as findings under the compliance factor.

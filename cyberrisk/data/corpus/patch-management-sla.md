# Patch Management Service Levels (synthetic guidance)

Vulnerabilities are prioritised by severity and by evidence of exploitation. Internet-facing systems carry the shortest deadlines because attackers scan them continuously.

Critical vulnerabilities with known exploitation must be patched or mitigated within seven days. Other critical vulnerabilities are remediated within fourteen days, high within thirty days, and medium within ninety days.

If a patch cannot be applied in time, apply a mitigation such as a virtual patch, network restriction or feature disablement, and record the residual risk. Verify the fix with a rescan and attach the result as evidence.

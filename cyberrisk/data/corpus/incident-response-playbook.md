# Incident Response Playbook (synthetic guidance)

An incident moves through detection, triage, containment, eradication, recovery and review. The first responder records the time of detection and opens an incident ticket with a severity estimate.

During containment, isolate affected hosts from the network but preserve volatile evidence. Do not wipe systems before forensic images are captured. Notify the legal and communications leads when customer data may be involved.

After recovery, hold a blameless review within ten working days. Track every corrective action as a remediation task with an owner and a due date.

# Vendor Security Baseline (synthetic guidance)

Every third-party vendor that processes company data must maintain a documented security programme. The programme names an accountable owner, defines minimum controls and is reviewed at least once a year.

Vendors rated critical must undergo a full assessment before onboarding and again every twelve months. Assessment evidence includes questionnaire answers, penetration test summaries and proof of independent certification where available.

When a vendor cannot meet a baseline control, a time-boxed exception must be recorded with a compensating control and an approver. Exceptions that remain open past their expiry date are escalated to the risk committee.
